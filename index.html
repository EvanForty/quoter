    Key differentiator

    Market making usually means hiring a trading firm with its own infrastructure and capital. Quoter turns it into a launchpad form: any token can run its own agent, funded by creator fees or a deposit.

    Proposed mechanismBuybacks are not a guarantee of price appreciation.

    Guardrails on every agent

    • No self-trading. An agent’s bids always sit below its asks, so it can never fill its own order.
    • No artificial volume. Agents only fill against outside takers.
    • Inventory cap. Bids stop once the position reaches your limit.
    • Daily volume cap. Quoting pauses for the day once the cap is hit.
    • Kill switch. A drawdown past your limit halts the agent until you restart it.
